# Paid Time Off Policy

## Accrual

| Years of service | PTO days per year |
|---|---|
| 0-2 | 18 |
| 3-5 | 22 |
| 6+ | 26 |

Full-time employees accrue PTO each pay period. Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into
the next calendar year; carried-over days expire on March 31.

## Requests

PTO requests of more than three consecutive days must be submitted two weeks in advance.

## Work From Home

Employees may work from home (WFH) up to two days per week with manager approval.
