# Travel and Expense Policy

Policy number: POL-FIN-017. Effective January 1, 2023. Superseded by the 2024 revision.

## Per Diem Rates

Employees travelling on approved business receive a daily per diem for meals and incidentals.

| City | Per diem (USD) | Lodging cap (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| New York | 85 | 320 |
| San Francisco | 85 | 320 |
| Other US cities | 65 | 180 |

## Receipts and Reimbursement

Receipts are required for any single expense above 50 USD. Expense reports must be submitted
within 45 days of trip completion.
