# Travel and Expense Policy

Policy number: POL-FIN-017. Effective January 1, 2024. Owner: Finance Operations.

## Per Diem Rates

Employees travelling on approved business receive a daily per diem for meals and incidentals.

| City | Per diem (USD) | Lodging cap (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| New York | 95 | 350 |
| San Francisco | 90 | 340 |
| Chicago | 80 | 260 |
| Other US cities | 70 | 200 |

## Approvals

Trips longer than five business days require approval from a director before booking.
International travel requires approval from the regional finance controller.

## Receipts and Reimbursement

Receipts are required for any single expense above 25 USD. Expense reports must be submitted
within 30 days of trip completion. Reimbursement is paid in the next payroll cycle.
